Arcana 1.6 — The Dead and the Siege

Released September 2026. Your launcher updates you when you reconnect.

The dead

  • THE DEAD WALK They come in from the sea at night, slow and many, and they hunt as one: noise and sight draw more of them. By day they're sluggish; by night they move with purpose. Most of what walks the island is now the dead. The island's own creatures keep to their home regions.
  • NO SKELETONS and far fewer of vanilla's other monsters.

Your home

  • THEY COME TO YOU The dead are drawn to homes. The bigger, louder, richer and brighter yours is, the more come, and in numbers they break through wood and glass fast, and stone slowly. Your Cornerstone tells you how strongly your home draws them. Nothing breaks while nobody is home.
  • FORTIFY Every block has a toughness. Greave's stone yard crew reinforces and repairs your walls.
  • DEFEND Turrets on your power grid (at home and at your wild workshops), barbed wire, barricades, spikes, alarms and floodlights. Nought sells slave soldiers to guard a home for fourteen days.

Towns

  • TOWNS HOLD The dead gather at the walls at night; archers on the wall walk shoot them down.
  • GOODS MOVE Towns produce, process and ship goods by wagon and boat. Other towns' potion shelves are now stocked by shipments. Haul a sealed crate yourself for coin, or take an escort job.

Arcana 1.5 — Gears and Current

Released September 2026. Your launcher updates you when you reconnect.

Engineering

  • A NEW PATH OF LEARNING Engineering is taught by masters across the island, one after another, starting with the Mechanist in Asteryn's crafts quarter. Each teaches what the next builds on.
  • POWER Hand cranks, water wheels, steam boilers and engines, turbines, wind, sun, and more, all measured in real watts and joules. Overloaded wires burn out; boilers can burst.
  • REAL WIRING Cables are chosen by copper size and voltage at a cable machine. Heavy machines run on three phases, and a distribution board shows you how to balance them. The Mechanist's manual explains it all in plain words.
  • MACHINES AND MULTIBLOCKS Furnaces, crushers, smelteries, presses, refineries and more, many of them large structures you build to a plan, with a ghost outline to guide you.
  • LOGISTICS Crates, item pipes, sorters and the Register: one screen onto everything you store.
  • THE LAND Prospect for ore and oil under the island and drill it out. Dirty power leaves haze and stained ground; reclaimers clean it, and blighted ground too.
  • BIGGER GOALS Power a town from your works and watch it light up; heal the island's blight; build a ship on your own slipway.

Towns

  • CURFEW After dark, towns expect you to have a room. Take one at the inn for the night, or rent one by the week with a locker. The watch walks everyone else out.

Bosses

  • MIDNIGHT Every story boss stands again at midnight (Italian time). A bell at each arena calls one back early, for a price that's never worth it for loot alone.
  • ARENAS You're only held in a boss's arena while you're really fighting it, and clearing one never locks you out of the others.

Classes

  • NEW PATHS Each class has a new branch built on its own strength: Rage, Focus, Wounds or the Stillpoint. Your old points are refunded, and every character gets one free respec.

Smaller things

  • Quivers can be widened at the Fletchery, and arrows are cheaper in bulk.
  • Your horse's speed shows while you ride, and your tamed horse comes home with you on Recall.
  • Engineering blocks drop when mined with the right tool.

Arcana 1.4 — Hearth and Hardship

Released September 2026. Your launcher updates you when you reconnect.

You can now put down roots out in the wild. Bosses got their real strength back, and they have a patience bar. And the wild no longer heals you: bring supplies.

Homes

  • A HOME OF YOUR OWN From level 10, buy a Cornerstone at Greave's stone yard (1,500 coin) and set it down at least 600 blocks from any town's wall. The square of ground around it is your claim. Holding the stone shows you where other people's claims are.
  • TAKING ROOT A new home takes root at the first dawn after a whole night.
  • WAKE AT HOME Pay 1,000 coin at Asteryn's city hall and a death or a Recall brings you home instead of to your town. Changing it back costs the same.
  • HOUSEHOLD Add friends at the stone; each can then bind their own wake to your home.
  • SAFE FROM CREATURES Nothing explodes a claim and nothing hostile is born on its doorstep. Players are on their honour.
  • ABANDON gives you the stone back; what you built stays.

Towns

  • TOWNS CAN'T BE GRIEFED Lava, water, fire, TNT, pistons, dispensers, boats, armour stands, frames, axes, hoes and bone meal no longer change a town. Doors, gates, levers, chests and townsfolk still work as before.

Bosses

  • FULL STRENGTHMinecraft capped every creature's health, so bosses had a fraction of their intended health and the same pool solo or in a party→Bosses now have their full health, scaled to your party. A boss takes about 3–5 minutes alone and 1–2 with a party of four.
  • PATIENCE A thin amber bar under a boss's health. It drains only when the fight isn't fair: the boss dragged out of its arena, nobody it can reach, or the boss stuck. When it empties, the boss goes home at full health and can be fought again straight away. Kiting on open ground is fine, and one player on a pillar can't reset a fight someone else is honestly tanking.
  • NO MORE WAIT The three-minute wait after a failed attempt is gone.

Healing and supplies

  • NO HEALING IN THE WILD Health no longer comes back on its own out in the wild. Inside a town's walls or your own claim you slowly mend out of combat. Food keeps you fed but no longer heals.
  • HEALING POTION Heals over time; one at a time. Sold at every general store, cheapest in Asteryn.
  • BANDAGE Stand still for a few seconds to apply it; a step or a blow spoils it. Fallow's tannery.
  • SALVE A slow heal you can walk with, out of combat. Crafted only.
  • MANA POTION Doubles a Mage's mana regeneration for a minute. Asteryn's alembic.
  • CRAFT YOUR OWN Every remedy can be made from plants that grow on the island.

Classes

  • MAGE — STILLPOINT Standing your ground in a fight floods your mana and quickens your spells. Stay planted behind someone who holds the enemy's attention.
  • ASSASSIN A kill now heals a share of your health, not all of it.
  • COMBAT Being hit now counts as being in combat for both sides, which fixes the Mage's ward refilling mid-fight and the Assassin's combat speed never turning on.

Arcana 1.2 — The League Update

Released September 2026. Every number in Arcana now reads the way League of Legends reads it: health in the hundreds on a bar marked every 100, Attack Damage, Bow Damage and Ability Power, Armor and Magic Resist, and a character sheet that shows exactly where every point comes from. Greave's forge becomes an item shop, spells scale with AP, and dying tells you what killed you. It also carries the fixes and the weapon rework made since 1.1.1.

Updating. If you play through the Arcana Prism instance, just launch it: it updates itself before the game starts. Otherwise import the new Arcana.zip from the pack's latest release.

Characters and the world carry over. Nothing is reset. The weapons and armour you carry are re-read with the new numbers when you log in; one left in a chest is re-read once it is in your pack.

Highlights

  • League's numbers. Health, damage, healing and shields read at League's scale: a level-1 Warrior has 700 health. Your bar has a line every 100 and a heavy line every 1,000, a shield is white, and other players' bars are blue.
  • Three damage stats. Attack Damage (melee), Bow Damage and Ability Power. A bow never runs on AP.
  • Armor and Magic Resist, League's rule. 100 halves that kind of damage. Every class has both, and they grow with level. Spells are magic damage, and some creatures resist them.
  • A character sheet that shows its work. Hover any stat in the Grimoire for your base and every bonus by name.
  • Spells scale with AP. The tooltip does the arithmetic for your character.
  • Greave's forge is an item shop. Tier 1 and 2 parts for coin, sell-back at 70 %, and eight named weapons with passives of their own.
  • A death recap, a boss-fight damage summary, a Tab scoreboard and a practice dummy in town.
  • Weapon tiers climb about five times from tier 1 to tier 5.

Numbers, the League way

  • THE SCALEHearts→League's scale. Every health, damage, healing and shield number you read is 25 times what it was, so nothing got stronger or weaker by it: a zombie that took a heart now takes 50 of your 700. Armor and Magic Resist are League's own numbers and are not scaled.
  • YOUR HEALTH BARHearts→A bar with a thin line every 100 health and a heavy line every 1,000, like League's. A shield (the Mage's ward, Rune Ward, a golden apple) is a white segment after your health.
  • OTHER PLAYERS→A blue bar over their heads, like allies in League. Creatures' bars stay red and get the same lines.
  • DAMAGE NUMBERS→Coloured by kind: orange physical, purple magic, white true, teal for arrows. A headshot is bigger, with a "!".
  • LOOK AT A TARGET→Its level, health, Armor, Magic Resist and the kind of damage it deals, in the target frame.

Your stats

  • ATTACK DAMAGE is melee damage, and says so everywhere.
  • BOW DAMAGE is its own stat: a plain arrow's damage is its base, levels and your bow's limb add to it, and a Blessing's percentage multiplies all of it. Ability Power no longer strengthens arrows.
  • ABILITY POWER starts at 0 and never grows with level, as in League. Every point is bought: a focus gives it, at twice what the stone used to add, so your spells deal what they did.
  • ARMOR AND MAGIC RESIST→Damage × 100 ÷ (100 + resist), League's rule, instead of vanilla's armour. Every class has both at level 1 and gains them each level (the Warrior most Armor, the Mage most Magic Resist). Vanilla armour still counts: full diamond is 120 Armor, 45 % less physical damage (vanilla made it 80 %).
  • PENETRATION→Lethality and Magic Penetration on some tier 3–5 edges and focus stones. The Stiletto's "ignores all armour", the Warhammer's Crush, the Wound's shred and Sunder are League penetration now.
  • ABILITY HASTE→Cooldown × 100 ÷ (100 + haste), on some tier 3–5 focus stones.
  • LEVELS Attack Damage and Bow Damage grow on League's back-loaded curve and land at level 20 exactly where they did. A Mage's level growth is on her spells' base damage now.
  • THE CHARACTER SHEET The Grimoire lists Health, Attack Damage (melee), Bow Damage, Ability Power, Armor, Magic Resist, Attack Speed, Ability Haste, penetration and Move Speed. A number raised by what you carry is gold. Hover it for the base (class and level), then every bonus by name (your weapon, each armour piece, each Blessing, your skill tree), and under Armor and Magic Resist how much damage you take.

Spells

  • ARCANE BOLT 275/308/341/374/407 (+50 % AP) magic damage. GRAVITY WELL +40 % AP, RUNE WARD +30 % AP. The rank raises the base; the ratio does not change with rank, as in League.
  • THE SPELL PAGE shows every rank with yours highlighted, then "For you: base + AP = total".

Creatures

Weapons and the Forge

What each kind of weapon does

A player asked for this in a sentence: "Can you make it so it clearly tells you what each type of the class weapon does? As in if there is any firing range difference or locking degrees."

The answers to his two questions, since they are now written down:

  • Locking on. The Mage's Arcane Bolt locks on to a creature within 15° of your aim and 14 blocks of the bolt — the same on Wand, Staff and Sceptre. Bows do not lock on at all.
  • Range. A bow's reach is how fast its arrows leave it. Bare, before any parts: the Shortbow draws fully in 0.77 s and shoots at plain-bow speed, the Longbow draws in 0.95 s at 1.10×, and the Warbow draws in 1.00 s at 1.34×. The Shuriken flies 12 blocks, through everything in its path.

What slots and traits are

  • A weapon has three slots. Core and Grip fit any weapon; the third is the weapon's own — Edge for a blade, Limb for a bow, Focus for a Mage's weapon.
  • On a bow, the core's damage is draw speed, the grip's speed is arrow speed, and the limb adds arrow damage. On a Mage's weapon the focus adds spell damage.

  • Traits come from parts. Each part carries one at most — a small spark marks the ones that do. A weapon holds three at most, and the same trait twice counts once.

  • A LOOSE PART'S DAMAGE AND SPEED "melee only" ⇒ "melee; draw speed on a bow" and "melee; arrow speed on a bow". It had been wrong since bows started reading those two numbers, and told Archers their best core did nothing.

  • Night is significantly brighter than 1.0.0, and can now be adjusted in game.
  • Towns can no longer be dug into. Fallow's protected area was too small and has been corrected.
  • The Path screen can be dragged. Parts of it were previously unreachable at common resolutions.

Mage

Arcane Bolt (Basic Ability)

  • MANA COST0→5
  • COOLDOWN0.6 seconds→1.1 seconds
  • DAMAGE6→11
  • SEEK CONE55 degrees→15 degrees

Damage per second is unchanged. The bolt now fires less often and hits harder, and a missed bolt costs mana.

The seek cone controls how far off-target a bolt may be loosed before it curves toward an enemy. At the previous value the cone was wider than the bolt's forward travel, meaning almost any bolt fired in the direction of an enemy would hit. Distance a shot may miss by and still connect:

  • AT 5 BLOCKS7.1 blocks→1.3 blocks
  • AT 8 BLOCKS11.4 blocks→2.1 blocks
  • AT 14 BLOCKS20.0 blocks→3.8 blocks

Arcane Bolt also applied the vanilla Glowing effect to anything it hit for 3 seconds. This was unintended and has been removed.

In 1.0.0 the brightness slider was capped below Minecraft's own "Moody" setting, which meant the brightness option had no useful range. The new cap is above Minecraft's default of 0.50.

Carried Light Smoothing (New)

Held torches, lanterns and other light sources previously moved between whole blocks as you walked, causing the surrounding light to step up and down several times a second.

  • LIGHT POSITIONSnapped to the block you stand in→Snapped to a grid 2 steps finer
  • SMOOTHING STEPSN/A→2 (adjustable, 1 to 16)

This affects what is drawn only. Creatures still calculate light in whole blocks, so a creature's view of whether it stands in light is unchanged.

New Commands

  • /arcanahud night [0..1] — sets night darkness. Applies immediately and is saved.
  • /arcanahud light [1..16] — sets carried light smoothing. Higher values are smoother and cost more performance. Applies immediately and is saved.

An NPC whose path to her station failed would remain where she stopped permanently. For a shopkeeper this appeared to the player as a permanently closed counter. NPCs now retry after one minute.

Quests

Spend a Skill Point

  • COUNTSPassives only→Passives and ability ranks

A skill point can be spent on a passive or on an ability rank. The step previously counted only passives, so a player who had spent their points on ability ranks could not complete it and had to reach the next level to gain another point.

This applies to existing characters immediately.
